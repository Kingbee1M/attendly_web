---
title: "Office Location & Geofencing Management"
---

The **Office Location** section enables administrators to create and manage office branches, define working shifts, schedule employees, configure GPS geofencing boundaries, and assign administrators to specific office locations.

<Note>
  **Location Access Restriction:** Admins assigned to specific branches will only see and manage the office locations tied to their administrator account. Super Administrators can assign single or multiple offices to any admin.
</Note>

---

## Office Location Directory Overview

![Office Location Directory](/images/Screenshot-2026-09-01-084659.png "Office Location Directory")

The main office directory displays all available branches along with action buttons to configure work parameters:

- **Copy ID:** Instantly copies the unique office identification key to the clipboard.
- **Search Input:** Search office locations by name or address details.
- **Manage Admin Offices:** Assign or unassign office locations for administrator accounts.
- **Create Office / Reset Device:** Actions to add new branch entries or manage device pairings.

| Column | Description |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Name** | Designated branch name (e.g., _Ile Ife Shop Mayfair_, _AKWA IBOM_, _Lagos_). |
| **Address** | Physical street address or landmark location of the office. |
| **Created At / Updated At** | System timestamps for record creation and last modification. |
| **Manage Shifts** | Opens the shift creation and agent assignment panel. |
| **Manage Work Schedule** | Opens shift roster scheduling for single or bulk employees. |
| **Action (Update)** | Opens the office configuration modal with map positioning and geofencing. |

Super Administrators can assign single or multiple office branches to individual administrators using the **Manage Admin Offices** modal:

1. Click the **Manage Admin Offices** button at the top right of the Office Location page.
2. **Select Admin:** Type the admin's name or email address in the **Search Admin** field and click on their profile from the list.
3. **Assign Offices:** Under the **Available Offices** section on the right, click **Assign** next to any branch you wish to grant the administrator access to.
4. **Remove Offices:** To revoke an assigned branch, locate the office under **Currently Assigned** on the left and click **Remove**.

Click **Manage Shifts** on any office row to create work shifts and assign local employees:

1. **Add New Shift:**
   - **Shift Name:** Enter a descriptive label (e.g., _Morning_).
   - **Start Time / End Time:** Select shift operating hours using the 30-minute increment dropdowns (e.g., `07:30 AM - 05:00 PM`).
   - Click **Add Shift** to save the shift definition.
2. **Existing Shifts & User Assignment:**
   - View created shifts for the location.
   - Click on available employees listed under **Assign Users** to toggle their assignment (`Assigned` status tags indicate active shift membership).
   - Edit or delete existing shift parameters as needed.

## Work Schedule Setup

![Work Schedule Setup](/images/Screenshot-2026-09-01-084914.png "Work Schedule Setup Modal")

Click **Manage Work Schedule** to build daily attendance rosters:

1. **Toggle Mode:** Choose between **Single Employee** or **Bulk Employees** setup.
2. **Employee Selection:** Search and select the target agent(s).
3. **Attendance Type:** Select **PRESENT** (in-office required) or **WFH** (Work From Home).
4. **Day Picker:** Select applicable working days (e.g., `MON`, `TUE`, `WED`, `THU`, `FRI`).
5. **Effective Date:** Pick the starting calendar date for the schedule rule.
6. Click **Create PRESENT Schedule** (or WFH Schedule) to apply.

## Office Geofencing & Map Location Setup

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Map Positioning">
    ![Update Office Location Map](/images/Screenshot-2026-09-01-084957.png "Update Office Location Map Modal")
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Geofence Radius">
    ![Geofence Radius Slider](/images/Screenshot-2026-09-01-085003.png "Geofence Radius Slider and Coordinates")
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Click the **Update** button on an office entry to configure map position and clock-in radius:

1. **Office Name & Address:** Enter or update the physical address details manually.
2. **Location Positioning:**
   - **Search Bar:** Type an address or landmark (e.g., _Ikeja City Mall_) to auto-locate on the map.
   - **My Location Button:** Click **My Location** to auto-detect and pinpoint your current GPS position.
   - **Map Pin / Coordinates:** Fine-tune position by dragging the pin directly on the interactive map or manually entering exact **Latitude** and **Longitude** values.
3. **Geofence Radius Slider:**
   - Adjust the slider to set the **Geofence Radius** (measured in meters, e.g., `390 meters`).
   - The blue circle visualizer on the map indicates the exact sphere of influence within which employees are permitted to clock in via the Attendly mobile app.
4. Click **Update Office** to save location and geofence settings.
